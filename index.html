Review and correct

Each line shows what was heard and what was shown. Type the correct English and save it. A short phrase goes into the church terms; a whole sentence goes into the prayer library. The fix applies from the next time it is heard.

Saved services

Every service is saved on this iPad by itself, from Start to Stop. The last 10 are kept; older ones are removed automatically. Tap Share to send one out, for example to email or Files.
